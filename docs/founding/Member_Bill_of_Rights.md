# The Member Bill of Rights

*Proposed under the Founding Charter of the Triarc — a founding proposal, not yet law. If ratified, these rights bind the Triarc itself: its panels, its moderators, its stewards, its founder, and its majorities.*

---

## Preamble

Every governance system eventually faces a majority that wants to silence, punish, or exclude an unpopular member. Rights written in that moment protect no one. These are written before it, so that they hold during it.

These rights are not granted by the Triarc. They are the conditions under which the Triarc is entitled to govern at all. The Founding Charter entrenches this document in full from the moment the community ratifies it: once ratified, it amends only at the entrenched threshold of Article VIII — a three-quarters majority with minimum participation of 40%.

---

## The Rights

**I. Voice.** Every Member may advocate any position, dissent from any decision, and criticise the Triarc itself — its rules, its stewards, its founder, its direction — without penalty of any kind. No sanction may ever attach to the content of a Member's positions.

**II. Vote.** Every Member holds one vote on every binding decision. No right, sanction, standing, contribution level, or period of inactivity may condition, dilute, or suspend that vote. Only membership revocation itself, under the due process of Right IV, may end it.

**III. Delegation.** Every Member may grant, withhold, revoke, or renew delegation freely, and may not be coerced, paid, or threatened into any delegation choice. Retaliation against a Member for how they voted or delegated violates this document.

**IV. Due process.** No Member may be sanctioned except by a Dispute Panel under Article VI of the charter, after notice of what is alleged, an opportunity to respond on the record, and a reasoned public decision. No moderator, steward, or founder may impose punishment summarily. Emergency interim measures must be time-limited and reviewed by a panel.

**V. Proportion.** Sanctions must be graduated: the mildest measure adequate to the harm. Warnings before suspensions, suspensions before revocation, and revocation only for the gravest or persistently repeated violations.

**VI. Identity.** No Member is required to disclose their legal identity to participate, and the Triarc will never publish it. Exposing a Member's outside identity against their will is among the gravest violations this community recognises. Verification may check government identification or financial records to confirm a Member is a real, unique person, and may never establish or reveal more than that pass/fail fact — not to the public, not to other Members, not to the Triarc's own governance.

**VII. Exit.** Every Member may leave at any time, without penalty, without process, and without forfeiting anything already distributed. Their record remains in the public history exactly as it stood.

**VIII. Petition.** Every Member may submit any proposal. The agenda may be scheduled by neutral procedure and never gatekept by content: no rule may allow any person or body to stop a proposal reaching deliberation because of what it argues.

**IX. Equality.** These rights belong identically to every Member — newest and oldest, most active and least, whatever they possess or lack outside the Triarc. Standing earns influence. It never earns extra rights, and nothing earns fewer.

**X. The Record.** Every Member has the right to the complete public record — every decision, vote, delegation, sanction, and dollar — and the right to have their dissent recorded permanently alongside any decision they opposed.

---

## Closing

A community that keeps these ten commitments can be trusted with everything else it builds. A community that breaks them has nothing left worth building.
